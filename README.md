# US E-Commerce Sales Analysis: Revenue Drivers & Performance Optimization

An end-to-end data analysis of US e-commerce transaction data to evaluate sales performance, identify key revenue drivers, analyze regional customer purchase behavior, and provide actionable recommendations for margin optimization.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

This project analyzes transactional e-commerce sales across various US regions and product categories. By combining Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), customer segmentation, and product margin evaluation, this analysis pinpoints profitability bottlenecks and outlines strategies to optimize promotional discounting and inventory allocation.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Answered

* **Revenue vs. Profitability:** Which product categories generate the highest gross revenue versus actual profit margins?
* **Discount Impact:** How do varying discount levels impact overall net profit across key product lines?
* **Geographic Trends:** Which US states/regions exhibit the highest customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and order volume?
* **Seasonal Demand:** What are the peak sales trends across months and quarters?
